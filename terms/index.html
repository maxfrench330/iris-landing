6. Content Moderation & Enforcement

Moderation on Iris is an active, ongoing part of how the Service operates, and it combines automated screening with human judgment. No enforcement action is ever taken by an algorithm acting alone.

6.1 Automatic scanning of every post

Every post submitted to Iris — every photo, video, and note — is automatically scanned at the moment of creation by Pangram, a third-party AI-text-detection service. Pangram analyzes the caption or note text associated with a post for indicators of AI-generated writing. This automatic scan does not, by itself, remove, label, or hide any post, and it does not notify the author that their post was scanned or flagged. The result of this scan is placed into a private queue for review by a human moderator, and no action is taken on the basis of the automated result alone.

6.2 Member-initiated "Report as AI-generated"

Separately from the automatic scan described above, any member may tap "Report as AI-generated" on a specific post they suspect was not authentically created. Doing so triggers an on-demand analysis using an Anthropic Claude AI model, which is capable of evaluating the image itself, not only associated text, and generates a report that is routed for human review.

6.3 General reporting

Members may also report any post by choosing a reason: spam, harassment, inappropriate content, AI-generated content, or something else. Reports of this kind, like AI-detection results, are placed into a queue for human review.
