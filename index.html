Why Iris

Closed Network

Iris is not open to the public. Members apply and are reviewed by a human. No mass sign-ups, anonymous accounts, or bots.

No Links In Bio

Posts live inside Iris exclusively. No external links, referral schemes, or redirecting your attention anywhere else.

Zero Ads. Ever.

Iris is member-supported. Devoid of advertisement campaigns, sponsored posts emulating real opinions, and brand deals dressed up as content.

No Fake Influencers

Paid promotions are prohibited. No links in bios. Just people with something real to say.

AI Content Policy

Moderators review every flag and take down AI-generated posts. Iris celebrates the irreplaceable beauty of human creativity.

Privacy By Default

No data exploitation, or algorithm manipulation. What happens on Iris stays inside Iris.
